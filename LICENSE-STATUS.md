# Licensing decision

The supplied workbook files do not contain an explicit overall reuse license grant. The LLM workbook does contain a specific CC BY-SA 4.0 notice for its AI Defense Matrix framework content; see [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md). Preparing this repository does not silently grant an overall license. The source files retain their existing rights and notices.

A concrete MIT license text is provided in [LICENSES/MIT.proposed.txt](LICENSES/MIT.proposed.txt) for the author's review of original material that can be licensed under those terms. It is a proposal, not the operative license. It cannot replace the existing attribution or the CC BY-SA 4.0 terms on covered third-party or adapted material. Before an open-source release, determine the component allocation and any applicable ShareAlike obligations, confirm the rights and intended scope, then add the appropriate license texts and update this page and the citation metadata.

MIT for separately identifiable original code and CC BY 4.0 for separately identifiable original documentation are possible choices, subject to the rights and component review. No blanket MIT or CC BY 4.0 allocation has been applied to the workbooks. The extent of ShareAlike coverage across the framework-derived worksheet and other workbook material has not been settled by this review.

For the meaning and effect of a repository license, see [GitHub's licensing guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository) and the [MIT text](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/mit/). Cited standards, research and third-party content retain their respective rights.
